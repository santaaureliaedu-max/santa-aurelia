# How to edit your website

No code needed. Everything here is done in your web browser.

## Signing in

1. Go to your website address and add `/admin` at the end.
   Example: `https://santaaurelia.vercel.app/admin`
2. Enter your email and password.
3. The very first time, you'll be asked to choose a new password. Pick something only you know and keep it safe.

## The editor

After signing in you'll see four sections you can change:

**Hero photos** — the big photos that slide across the top of the homepage.
**Gallery** — the photo grid, each with a caption.
**Teachers** — a photo, name, and role for each teacher.
**Logos** — the two logos in the top corner.

## Changing a photo

- Click **Upload** next to any photo, choose a picture from your computer, and it appears.
- Or paste a web link into the "Image URL" box if the photo is already online.

## Changing text

- Click inside a caption, name, or role box and type. That's it.

## Reordering

- Grab the **⠿** handle on the left of any item and drag it up or down.
  The order you see is the order visitors will see.

## Adding or removing

- **+ Add** at the bottom of a section adds a new empty item.
- **Remove** deletes that item.

## Saving

- When you're happy, click **Save all changes** at the bottom.
- To throw away changes you haven't saved, click **Discard changes**.
- After saving, open your website in a new tab to see it live.

## Tips

- Photos look best when they're roughly square for teachers, and wide (landscape) for the hero and gallery.
- If you make a mistake, you can always re-upload or edit again — nothing is permanent until you save, and you can change it any time.
- Keep your password private. Anyone with it can edit the site.
