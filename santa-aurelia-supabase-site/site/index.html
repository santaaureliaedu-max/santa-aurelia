About us

Nearly 30 years in one loving community

Since 1997, Sekolah Santa Aurelia has cared for the children of Kelapa Gading — right across from BPK Penabur. Generations of families have grown up with us, and many parents who came here as children now bring their own.

Our vision

To raise a generation that is intelligent, warm-hearted, and grounded in strong moral character.

Our mission

To make school joyful — where children learn through play, in a safe environment guided by dedicated, experienced teachers.

Teachers you can trust

Our teachers are S1-qualified, and several have been named guru teladan (model teacher) across DKI Jakarta. Many have taught here for more than 20 years — the same familiar, caring faces year after year.
